About Alicia

A voice bridging spirituality, media and travel.

Waking up a little more each day — to presence, to gratitude, and to a real connection with what surrounds us.

Alicia Gorbato is a media professional and mindfulness teacher based in India, where she combines on-camera work with a deep spiritual practice and a life devoted to animal rescue. Through @despertares_con_alicia she shares daily content on wellness, consciousness and mindful living, building a loyal and steadily growing community that trusts her recommendations on brands, destinations and experiences.
