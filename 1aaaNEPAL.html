Standing with Nepal after the Bhote Koshi flash floods

On August 26, 2026, a catastrophic glacial-lake flash flood swept down from Tibet through Nepal's Rasuwa and Nuwakot districts — destroying homes, bridges, and hydropower infrastructure. Communities need urgent help, and sustained support in the months ahead.

What happened

A sudden flash flood tore down the Bhote Koshi river from the Tibetan side of the border, sweeping through Timure and Syabrubesi before surging into the Trishuli river system and triggering high alerts across Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha, and Chitwan districts. Roads and bridges have been cut off, complicating rescue efforts, and thousands remain affected as search-and-rescue and needs assessments continue.

Relief organizations warn that once the immediate rescue phase passes, Nepal will need sustained support for rebuilding homes, restoring infrastructure, and mental health care — a recovery process that, after past floods, has taken months to years.

Ways to collaborate beyond donating

Volunteer with local NGOs

If you're in or near the affected districts, local and government-coordinated relief efforts need help with shelter management, relief distribution, and medical support.

Amplify verified updates

Share confirmed information from official sources (government, IFRC, established NGOs) rather than unverified reports — misinformation actively slows down relief efforts.

Support the long recovery

Rebuilding homes, infrastructure, and providing mental health support will take months to years. Consider recurring donations or checking back with relief organizations later this year.

Skills-based help

Designers, developers, translators, and logistics volunteers can often plug into diaspora and NGO coordination efforts — reach out directly to the organizations above to ask how to contribute skills.
