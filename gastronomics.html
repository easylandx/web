What makes Culichi Sushi different?

Culichi Sushi comes from Culiacán, Sinaloa — where cooks took the sushi roll and made it entirely their own. Instead of raw fish and a light touch of soy sauce, the rolls are breaded, fried or baked, piled with melted cheese, and finished with the sauces and heat of Sinaloan cooking.

At Ana Gastronomics, that's exactly the tradition we cook from. Every roll is prepared fresh to order, built for people who want big flavor in every bite — not a downsized, Americanized version of the dish.
