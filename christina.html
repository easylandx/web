Meet Christina

Bilingual psychotherapist & embodied sex educator

I’m Christina Hidalgo, a first-generation Latina and Licensed Clinical Social Worker based in Austin/Houston, Texas. You are the expert on yourself. Together we’ll build a safe, trusting space to explore your concerns, strengths, beliefs, needs, desires and goals.

I identify within the LGBTQ+, queer, non-monogamous and kink communities, and I’m competent to support others in this space. As a woman of color, I’m driven by social justice and advocate for women’s, LGBTQIA+ and human rights.
